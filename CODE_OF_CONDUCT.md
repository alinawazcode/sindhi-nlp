# Code of conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

- Be respectful and welcoming. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith, and give constructive, specific feedback.
- Harassment, personal attacks and discrimination are not acceptable.
- Sindhi language and its speakers deserve care: do not use the project to produce or promote hateful content about any community.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or block contributors who break these rules. Report problems to alinawaz.code@gmail.com.
